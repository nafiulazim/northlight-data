Services

What we build for you

Every project starts with one question: what decision does this data need to support? Then we build exactly that: dashboards, reporting and practical AI.

Interactive dashboards

Power BI, Tableau, Looker Studio or web dashboards with filters, drill-downs and clear KPIs.

Data cleaning & structuring

Messy spreadsheets and survey exports turned into tidy, reliable datasets ready for analysis.

Analysis & insights

Trends, comparisons and key findings, explained in plain language with charts that tell the story.

Automated reporting

Weekly, monthly or donor reports that update themselves, saving your team hours every cycle.

KPI & results tracking

Sales targets and KPIs for businesses; indicators, logframes and donor reporting for programmes.

Infographics & data visuals

Publication-ready charts, one-pagers and visual summaries for reports and presentations.
